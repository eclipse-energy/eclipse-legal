Delete your account in the app

You can permanently delete your account at any time directly within the app.

To delete your account, navigate to:

  1. Account (Settings)
  2. Profile
  3. Delete Account

After confirming the deletion, your account and all associated personal data will be permanently removed from the Eclipse App. This action cannot be undone.

If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
