Services

Three services. One clear picture of your energy.

Eclipse brings together your smart meter, your home devices and your power peaks, so you know where your energy goes and when.

2027New grid fees in Austria

Power Analytics: see your peaks before they count.

From 2027, peaks start to matter: part of your grid fee will depend on your strongest quarter hour of the month. Keep scrolling to see on an example day how Eclipse makes your peaks visible.

From 1 January 2027, households in Austria also pay a power price. It is based on your highest 15-minute peak each month; grid areas switch over by 1 July 2028 at the latest. Power Analytics shows when your peaks happen, so you can spread out the load from your EV, heat pump or oven.

Source: E-Control, Systemnutzungsentgelte-Grundsatzverordnung, BGBl. II Nr. 299/2026

  • Overview: your highest 15-minute power, power trend and unusual peaks
  • Times: heatmap of when your load rises, plus your typical daily load
  • Frequency: set your own peak limit and see how often you exceed it
  • Daily detail: your three highest readings of any day

Eclipse analyses your power peaks. It does not calculate grid costs.

How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Set up once, then Eclipse keeps your energy data up to date.

  1. Create your account

    Download Eclipse for iOS or Android and sign up with your email address.

  2. Connect your smart meter

    Add your residence and enter your metering point number, then grant access to your smart meter data.

The ECLIPSE project

Part of a European movement for smarter energy use.

The Eclipse app is developed by FH Upper Austria within ECLIPSE (Energy Consumption reduction based on Open-source Reference Framework). The project empowers consumers across Europe to reduce and shift their energy consumption, using transparent data on savings, CO₂ and financial incentives.
