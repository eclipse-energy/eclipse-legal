This page provides information on how to contact us for support regarding the mobile application Eclipse (“the app”), provided by FH OÖ Forschungs & Entwicklungs GmbH (FH-Hagenberg) (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

1.General Support

If you experience technical issues, have questions about features, or need assistance using the app, please contact us via email.

eclipse@fh-hagenberg.at

Please include:

  • your device model
  • iOS version
  • app version
  • a short description of the issue
  • screenshots if applicable

This helps us resolve your request faster.

2.Bug Reports

If you encounter a bug or unexpected behavior, please provide:

  • steps to reproduce the issue
  • expected behavior
  • actual behavior
  • any relevant screenshots

Send reports to:

eclipse@fh-hagenberg.at

3.Feature Requests

We welcome feedback and ideas for improvements.

If you have suggestions for new features or enhancements, feel free to contact us at:

eclipse@fh-hagenberg.at

4.Research Context

The app is developed as part of a research and development project.
Response times may vary depending on project workload and academic schedules.

We appreciate your understanding.

5.Legal Inquiries

For formal or legal inquiries, please contact:

eclipse@fh-hagenberg.at
